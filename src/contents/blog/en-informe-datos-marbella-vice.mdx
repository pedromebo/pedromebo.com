## Introduction

Marbella Vice is a GTA RP server founded by *Ibai Llanos* and *Jacky* (Coolifegame) aimed at content creators. The server was developed by the *MoleCorp* team, bringing all their experience and customization capabilities. The creators were directly invited through a passport system or via applications.

The entry process for creators was not the same for everyone, as they joined at different dates.

This project measures the data from the moment a creator's entry is publicly announced by their community or themselves. This means that some creators might not be included in the lists, and thus their content has not been monitored.

Unlike most Twitch analytics platforms, this project operated with a 1-minute refresh rate (data collection frequency), providing higher granularity and precision. Nevertheless, these metrics are custom-calculated and *not provided directly by Twitch*, meaning that while they strive to be as close to reality as possible, slight variations may exist.

## General Data

### Total Viewers
An estimated total of **26,949,099 non-unique viewers**:
- **From 04/11 to 05/10:** 15,065,572 viewers.
- **From 05/11 to 06/21:** 11,883,527 viewers.

### Total Hours Watched
A total of:
#### **113,531,617 hours.**
That is **4,730,484 days**, equivalent to **12,960 years**.

### Total Hours Created
Across **6,921 live streams**, a total of:
#### **29,557 hours.**
That is **1,231 days**, equivalent to **3.37 years**.

If you planned to watch all of Marbella Vice's broadcasted content at 8 hours a day, it would take you **10 years and 43 days**.
